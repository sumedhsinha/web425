About HaxScore

Exploring hackathons and gear since 2023. A passionate team dedicated to providing authentic, detailed reviews and insights into the world of competitive programming events and cutting-edge technology.

Our Mission

To create a comprehensive resource for evaluating hackathons and gear through rigorous testing, honest feedback, and community collaboration. We believe in transparency and detailed analysis.

Our Vision

Build a trusted platform where participants can make informed decisions about which hackathons to attend and what equipment will best serve their needs in competitive environments.

Our Values

Integrity in every review, inclusivity in our community, and a commitment to helping others succeed. We stand by our ratings and methodology.
