A Guide From The Floor

YHack 2026 - Photo by Sumedh Sinha (March 2026)

HaxScore is a practical guide for people who want to survive and excel at hackathons. The site combines first-hand event reviews, honest gear analysis, and visual breakdowns of what actually happens during a 24 to 48-hour sprint. As the site evolves, you will find venue ratings, mentorship quality notes, hardware and API availability, travel logistics, and performance-focused equipment writeups that help you decide what is worth carrying into a high-pressure weekend. What makes HaxScore different is that every insight is grounded in direct attendance, real build sessions, and post-event reflection instead of marketing copy. This is written by hackers, for hackers, with a focus on outcomes: learn faster, build better, and leave each event stronger than you arrived.
