Our Ways & Methods

How HaxScore Guarantees Unbiased, High-Quality Reviews

At HaxScore, transparency is our top priority. You deserve to know exactly how we arrive at our conclusions and star ratings.

We do not accept paid placements for positive reviews. If an event sponsor provides us with travel accommodations or a tech company sends us a review unit, it will be explicitly disclosed at the top of the article. Otherwise, assume we paid our own way and bought the gear with our own money.

The HaxScore Star System

We use a strict 5-star rating scale across all of our reviews to maintain consistency:

  • (Masterpiece): Flawless execution. A must-attend event or a must-buy product that sets the industry standard.
  • (Great): Highly recommended. Minor flaws that do not significantly detract from the overall experience.
  • (Average): Functional and fine, but unremarkable. Fills a need but has noticeable drawbacks.
  • (Poor): Hard to recommend. Serious issues with execution, build quality, or event organization.
  • (Avoid): A complete failure. Do not spend your time or money here.
