Sony Continues To Deliver with The XM series

A Review of the WH-1000XM4 ($349.99)

Sumedh Sinha | Published: March 25, 2026 1:25 pm | Updated: March 25, 2026 6:03 pm

Who Is It For

  • Frequent travelers (for stress free flights)
  • Remote workers and students (who require long battery life)
  • Casual listeners (who want rich and bass-forward sound)

If you have the XM3s, then this generation is skippable.

Rating: 4.5 out of 5 (****1/2)

Pros/Cons

+ Great all-around pick for most people

+ Excellent noise isolation and cancelation

+ Decent build quality while being portable (and foldable)

- Messy control app (its quite clunky and slow)

- Call quality is average (some bugs with the mics)

- Pause to Speak is too sensitive (most would probably end up disabling it)

Links to Buy

  • Amazon
  • BestBuy
  • Sony

Overview

The Sony WH1000-XM4 (hereafter referred to as XM4s) sits at a sweet spot in the premium noise-canceling headphones market. Compared to competitors, the XM4 aims to be a well rounded offering rather than excelling in just one area. It doesn't have the absolute best call quality (Bose QC series) or the most premium build (Apple AirPod Max), but it delivers one of the most complete experiences in the category. It comes in 3 colors: Black, Blue and Silver.

Building on the well received XM3s, Sony has managed to elevate this new generation further by enhancing the ANC system (active noise cancelation), adding multi-device support and tuning audio quality. These are easily one of the best headphones on the market, and quite frequently feature as the #1 in their segment.

Features

  • Multipoint Connection: Allows you to stay connected to your laptop and smartphone simultaneously. The headphones will intelligently switch audio based on the activity.
  • Speak-to-Chat: A proximity-based feature that pauses your music and enables transparency mode the moment you start speaking.
  • Wear Detection: A hidden sensor in the left ear cup detects when you remove the headphones, instantly pausing playback to save battery.
  • LDAC Support: High-resolution wireless audio support for Android users, delivering up to 990 kbps of data.
  • Custom EQ: Accessible via the Sony Connect app, allowing you to tame the bass or boost the treble to your specific liking.

Performance Tests

Several reputable outlets conducted in-depth tests, and here are some of their key findings:

  • Active Noise Cancellation (ANC): The XM4s excel in neutralizing low-frequency sounds. Technical measurements show it can attenuate noise by up to 30dB in the 75-100Hz range (Molina, 2024). In my experience, it has been extremely effective at vanishing the low hum of airplane engines and HVAC systems.
  • Audio Fidelity: Out of the box, the XM4s have a warm and bass-forward signature. Frequency response charts indicate a significant hump in the upper-bass (approx. 150Hz), which some listeners find "muddy" (Audio Science Review, 2021). However, the adjustable EQ is highly effective at flattening the response for a more balanced, audiophile-grade sound. You just need to get to that section within the rather outdated app.
  • Battery Life: While the box claims 30 hours, real-world stress tests with ANC enabled at 75dB typically result in 19 to 24 hours of playback (SoundGuys, 2024). It supports Quick Charge, providing roughly 5 hours of use from a 10-minute charge.
  • Microphone Quality: The XM4s use five microphones for its Precise Voice Pickup technology. While it provides clear enough audio for indoor Zoom calls, it struggles with wind isolation so it wouldn't be ideal to use it on the go. I have been forced to disconnect and use the laptop/phone microphones in some scanerios due to this issue.

The Verdict

While not flawless, the XM4s represent one of the best offerings in the headphone market. If you want a headphone that excels in everyday usability, travel, and all-round performance, the XM4s are one of the safest and smartest buys on the market. I'll be keeping mine for years to come!

The New Apple MacBook Pro Is A Beast

The Most Premium Laptop on the Market ($1599 - $7249 est. confg. dependent)

Sumedh Sinha | Published: March 25, 2026 1:25 pm | Updated: March 25, 2026 6:03 pm

Who Is It For

  • Developers compiling large codebases (especially for AI workloads)
  • Video editors, 3D artists, and creative professionals
  • Power users needing high sustained performance with good battery life

M3 MacBook Pro users can skip this generation if they wish so.

Rating: 5 out of 5 (*****)

Pros/Cons

+ Unmatched build quality

+ Extravagant 120 Hz display

+ Best in-class performance and AI-ready

- Camera still needs work

- RAM pricing not as competitive

Links to Buy

  • Amazon
  • BestBuy
  • Microcenter

Overview

The MacBook Pro M4 Pro (Nov 2024) represents Apple's continued dominance in the high-performance laptop space. Rather than redesigning the MacBook, Apple focuses on what matters most: massive performance gains, efficiency, and AI-ready computing. It comes in two sizes: 14 and 16 inches. There are 2 color variants: Silver and Space Grey. This review will focus on the 14 inch variant (spec'd with M4 Pro 14-core, 24 GB of RAM and 1 TB SSD storage).

Ever since Apple ventured into making its own chipset in the form of M-series, the response has been immensely positive. Nearly everyone loves the performance upgrades that have come after the Intel MacBook debacles from 2016. Apple has also kept the familiar traditional design and the reliable Magic keyboard (we have finally left Butterfly(ies) in the rear view mirror - good riddance!).

Compared to competitors like Dell XPS, Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Extreme, and ASUS Zephyrus, Apple leads in performance-per-watt, battery life, and thermal efficiency. Combined with its build quality, exceptional display quality and paucity of connectivity options, this is a truly unmatched workhorse. The trackpad in the 16 inch is bigger than my entire iPhone 13 Pro Max (truly insane)!

Features

  • Apple M4 Chipset: Built on 3nm technology, featuring up to a 16-core CPU and 40-core GPU (on Max models) with hardware-accelerated ray tracing. It is absolutely trailblazing.
  • 120 Hz Display: The Liquid Retina XDR screen is capable of upto 120 Hz refresh rate. The colors pop so nicely, and the image remains sharp in nearly all conditions.
  • Thunderbolt 5: Available on M4 Pro and Max models, offering up to 120Gb/s of data transfer (essential for multi-8K monitor setups). Super quick, and super smooth.
  • Apple Intelligence: A dedicated Neural Engine optimized for local LLM processing and advanced image generation tools. Convenient, but still missing out some major promises.

Performance Tests

Several reputable outlets conducted in-depth tests, and here are some of their key findings:

  • CPU Benchmarks: In Geekbench 6 tests, the M4 Max achieved a single-core score of 3,885 and a multi-core score of 25,650, representing about a 20% improvement over the M3 Max (Geekbench, 2024). In Cinebench 2024, the M4 Pro delivered roughly 177 points in single-core performance, which remains competitive with high-end desktop chips in its class (NotebookCheck, 2024).
  • GPU & Graphics: The 20-core GPU in the M4 Pro scored approximately 113,590 in Metal benchmarks, nearly doubling the performance of the base M4 chip (Shephard, 2024). This allows for fluid real-time rendering in apps like Octane or Blender.
  • Battery Endurance: Real-world testing shows the M4 Pro 14-inch lasting roughly 19 to 22 hours for wireless web browsing. Under heavy sustained load (video editing), this drops to about 5-7 hours, which is still industry-leading.
  • SSD Speed: Higher-tier 2TB models recorded sequential read speeds of 6,728 MB/s, making it one of the fastest internal storage systems in a laptop to date (Shephard, 2024).

The Verdict

The MacBook Pro M4 is arguably the most "complete" machine in this era of computing. It addressed the RAM and connectivity complaints of the M3 and provides a display option that no competitor has yet matched in terms of color accuracy. This machine will continue to remain an inspiration for other companies, and a beloved powerhouse for its users. Coming from the M1 MacBook Air 2020, this machine has been truly incredible for my workflow and efficiency.
